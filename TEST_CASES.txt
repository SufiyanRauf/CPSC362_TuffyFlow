What is not tested, and why
---------------------------

Worth saying plainly, because "%d cases, all passing" invites the reader to assume more
than it should. There are no automated tests for the React screens, the sign in flow or
the map. The logic those screens depend on was deliberately moved out of them, into
src/lib and api/, so that it could be tested without a browser harness, and that is what
the cases below cover. What is left in the components is layout and wiring, and it is
checked by hand.

Two consequences are worth naming rather than leaving for a reader to find. FR-1 and
FR-2 are proved at the database rather than at the screen: the evidence is that the
rules accept a good write and refuse a bad one, not that the form submits. And the demo
mode, which lets somebody try the app with no account, reads bundled rows instead of
Firestore, so no security rules case can reach it; it is checked by hand too.
